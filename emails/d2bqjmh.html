The Lab

This one is free, and it is for anyone with a kid, a classroom, or a curious streak. It is a library of real experiments you can run with what is already on a farm or in a kitchen: shake soil in a jar and watch it settle, float an egg to tell its age, put a thermometer in the compost, see which flour makes a sourdough starter rise highest. Every one ends with something written down, so it is an experiment and not a chore.
